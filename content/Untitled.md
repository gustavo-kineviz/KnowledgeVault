# Vibe Coding Presentation Script

_Total time: ~10 minutes_

## 🎬 **SLIDE 1: Title (30 seconds)**

**Opening hook:** "What if I told you that creating complex graph visualizations could be as easy as having a conversation? Today I want to show you something that's changing how we think about development."

**Transition:** _Click to next slide_

---

## 🎯 **SLIDE 2: The Problem (45 seconds)**

**Pain point introduction:** "Let's start with something we all know too well. You're experts at data visualization, you work with complex graph data every day, but..."

**Read the slide text, then add:** "Sound familiar? You know exactly what insights you want to extract, but you end up spending most of your time fighting with APIs instead of exploring the data."

**Transition:** "But what if we could change this?"

---

## 💡 **SLIDE 3: What We're Exploring (30 seconds)**

**Solution preview:** "Today I'm going to show you vibe coding with Grove. Instead of learning complex API syntax, you just describe what you want in plain English."

**Pause for effect:** "Natural language goes in, working Grove code comes out. And yes, there's a demo video coming to Slack soon that'll show this in action!"

---

## 🔄 **SLIDE 4: Demo Comparison (60 seconds)**

**Traditional way:** "Let's look at how this works. On the left, we have the traditional approach - you need to know Grove's API, write loops, handle all the edge cases..."

**Point to the code:** "This is just a simple example, but imagine doing this for complex graph operations."

**Vibe coding way:** "On the right, vibe coding. You just describe what you want, and AI generates the proper Grove code with correct GraphXR API calls."

**Key point:** "Same result, completely different process."

---

## 🎯 **SLIDE 5: How This Solves Pain (60 seconds)**

**Read through the benefits:** "Here's how this addresses each pain point step by step..."

**Emphasize each point:**

- "No more memorizing API documentation"
- "Iterate faster by changing descriptions, not rewriting code"
- "Focus on what matters - the insights"
- "Domain experts can directly translate their knowledge"

**Transition:** "But there's a critical principle behind making this work..."

---

## 🦾 **SLIDE 6: Iron Man Analogy (75 seconds)**

**Set up the analogy:** "Andrej Karpathy has this great quote about AI partnership..."

**Read the quote with emphasis:** "We need more Iron Man suits, fewer Iron Man robots."

**Explain the difference:** "Left side - autonomous AI doing everything. High risk, no oversight, black box decisions."

"Right side - human-AI partnership. You're still in control, AI amplifies your capabilities."

**Key takeaway:** "AI does the generation, humans do the verification. This is the sweet spot."

---

## 🔄 **SLIDE 7: Understanding Vibe Coding (45 seconds)**

**Clarification:** "Let me be clear - vibe coding isn't about replacing programming skills. You're still defining problems, making architectural decisions, building systems."

**The difference:** "You're just using a more natural interface. Instead of speaking the computer's language, the computer speaks yours."

---

## ✅ **SLIDE 8: Best Practices (60 seconds)**

**Do's:** "If you're going to try vibe coding, here are the golden rules. Use Git religiously - AI can make mistakes. Review everything, test everything, choose the right tool for each job."

**Don'ts:** "And the pitfalls to avoid - never blindly trust AI outputs. Don't become dependent. You still need to understand your system."

**Transition:** "So what's the real impact here?"

---

## 🚀 **SLIDE 9: Real Impact (45 seconds)**

**Broader perspective:** "This isn't just about writing less code. We're democratizing graph development."

**Read through the impact for each role:**

- Data scientists, domain experts, developers, teams

**Transition:** "Let me wrap up what we've covered..."

---

## 📊 **SLIDE 10: What We Solved (30 seconds)**

**Summary:** "We've shown how vibe coding with Grove eliminates API learning curves, speeds up iteration, and lets you focus on insights instead of syntax."

**Teaser:** "And there's more Grove capabilities coming in that demo video!"

---

## 🎭 **SLIDE 11: Setup for Reveal (30 seconds)**

**Build suspense:** "Now, throughout this presentation, I've been showing you how AI can transform graph development, how natural language can replace complex coding..."

**Pause:** "But there's something I haven't told you yet..."

---

## 🎯 **SLIDE 12: The Big Reveal (60 seconds)**

**The reveal:** "This presentation was created using vibe coding."

**Process explanation:** "I described what I wanted to Claude and shared my Obsidian files - the same ones I use for my Knowledge Vault."

**The iteration part:** "It took 5 iterations and multiple revisions to get it right."

**Connect back:** "Perfect example of the Iron Man analogy we love in action."

---

## 🚀 **SLIDE 13: Call to Action (45 seconds)**

**Encouragement:** "Your turn to experiment. Try vibe coding your next Grove task. Use AI for boilerplate, but always review the results."

**Community building:** "Most importantly, join our Discord discussions. I'm posting tech insights there regularly, and it's a great place for us to build a community around these ideas."

**Closing:** "Questions? Let's discuss!"

# 📱 **HOW TO PRESENT WITH CAMERA + SCREEN SHARE**

## **Setup Options:**

### **Option 1: Dual Monitor Setup (Recommended)**

- **Monitor 1:** Presentation in full screen for screen sharing
- **Monitor 2:** Script document open for you to read
- Camera positioned so you can glance at script naturally

### **Option 2: Single Monitor with Phone**

- Open script on your phone
- Position phone where you can glance down naturally
- Presentation full screen on computer for sharing

### **Option 3: Presenter Notes**

- Copy key bullet points to sticky notes
- Place around your monitor edges
- Use as prompts rather than reading verbatim

## **Technical Tips:**

### **Before Starting:**

- Test screen share + camera combo
- Make sure script is easily readable
- Practice transitioning between slides smoothly
- Have water nearby

### **During Presentation:**

- **Eye contact:** Look at camera when making key points
- **Natural glances:** It's okay to look at notes, just don't read obviously
- **Pace yourself:** Pause between slides, let points land
- **Use space bar:** For smooth slide transitions

### **Body Language:**

- **Gestures:** Point to screen elements when explaining
- **Posture:** Sit up straight, lean slightly forward (engaged)
- **Movement:** Small head movements to emphasize points

## **Script Usage Tips:**

1. **Don't read verbatim** - use as bullet points
2. **Practice transitions** between slides 2-3 times
3. **Mark emphasis points** in your script
4. **Have backup phrases** if you lose your place
5. **Time yourself** - aim for 9-10 minutes to leave room for questions

## **Emergency Phrases:**

- "Let me show you what I mean..." (if you need a moment)
- "The key thing here is..." (to refocus)
- "This is where it gets interesting..." (transition phrase)

**You've got this! The content is solid, just be conversational and enthusiastic about the topic.** 🚀
